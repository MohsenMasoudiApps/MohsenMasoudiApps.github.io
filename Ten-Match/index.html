Ten Match – Number Puzzle ("Ten Match", "we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how your information is handled when you use our application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

No Account Required

Ten Match does not require users to create an account, register, sign in, or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number to play the game.

Local Game Data

Your game progress, scores, statistics, active sessions, settings, and achievements are stored locally on your device using SQLite and Android DataStore.

We do not operate our own backend servers or maintain a remote database of player profiles or game activity.

Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the App. This does not necessarily delete information independently collected by third-party advertising providers.

2. Device Permissions

Ten Match requests only the permissions necessary for its functionality and advertising services.

  • INTERNET: Allows Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements when an internet connection is available.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the App and advertising SDK to check network connectivity.
  • VIBRATE: Provides optional haptic feedback during gameplay. Vibration can be disabled in the App settings.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, SMS messages, or personal files.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Ten Match is supported by advertisements provided through Google AdMob.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use Google's Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements within the App.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Device information and operating system details.
  • Advertising interactions and diagnostic information.
  • Information used for advertising measurement and fraud prevention.

The collection and processing of this information are governed by Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Advertising Information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Advertising Consent (UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Ten Match uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present applicable privacy and advertising consent choices.

Where required, users can review, manage, or withdraw their advertising consent through the available privacy options in the App.

Advertising Experience

  • Banner advertisements are displayed in designated menu areas.
  • Interstitial advertisements are restricted to natural transitions between gameplay sessions.
  • Full-screen advertisements are not intentionally displayed during active puzzle-solving sessions.
  • Interstitial advertisements are subject to frequency limits, including a minimum three-minute cooldown and a maximum of two advertisements per session.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

Third-party advertising services may process certain device information as described in this Privacy Policy. Advertising configuration and consent requirements are applied according to applicable laws and Google Play policies.
