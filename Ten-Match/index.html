This Privacy Policy explains how Ten Match - Number Puzzle ("Ten Match", "the App", "we", or "our") handles information when you use the game.

1. Information Collection and Storage

No Account Required

Ten Match does not require users to register, create an account, or sign in. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or phone number to play.

Local Game Data

Your game progress, active sessions, high scores, statistics, settings, and achievements are stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a custom backend or remote player-profile system for this game data.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. This does not necessarily delete information already processed by third-party providers.

2. Device Permissions

The App may use the following device capabilities:

  • Internet access: To request and display advertisements.
  • Network connectivity: To check whether a network connection is available.
  • Vibration: To provide optional haptic feedback during gameplay.

The App does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, SMS messages, or personal files for its core game functionality.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Ten Match uses Google AdMob advertising to support the free availability of the game.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising and device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Advertisement impressions, clicks, and interactions.
  • Advertising and diagnostic information.

The information processed depends on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable requirements.

The App may display banner advertisements in designated menu areas and interstitial advertisements at natural transitions between game sessions.

Optional rewarded advertisements may be offered in exchange for an in-game benefit, where available.

We aim to avoid interrupting active gameplay. Actual advertisement availability and presentation may depend on network conditions, ad inventory, consent, and the current App version.

Ten Match is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
