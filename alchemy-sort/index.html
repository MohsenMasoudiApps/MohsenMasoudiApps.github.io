A. Personal Data

The App does not require user registration, login, an account, an email address, a name, or a phone number. You can play the game without creating an account or providing these details.

B. Device and Usage Data

To support advertising and app functionality, the App integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google Play Services. Depending on the services enabled, device settings, and consent choices, these services may automatically process information such as:

  • Device identifiers and technical information: Advertising ID (where available), IP address, device model, and operating system version.
  • Approximate location: An approximate region, such as country or city-level location, may be inferred from the IP address by Google services.
  • Diagnostics and performance information: Crash reports, app launch or response information, ANR diagnostics, and other performance metrics.

We do not use this information to identify you directly. Information handled by Google services is subject to Google's privacy practices.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the integrated services may be used to:

  1. Provide, maintain, and improve the game's features and performance.
  2. Display and measure personalized or non-personalized advertisements through Google AdMob.
  3. Provide optional in-game rewards, such as bonus coins or undo moves, when you choose to watch a rewarded video advertisement.
  4. Diagnose, prevent, and fix technical issues, crashes, and performance problems.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses services provided by Google LLC. These services may process information as described in their own privacy documentation:

  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy

Google's collection and use of information is governed by Google's applicable terms, policies, and settings.

4. In-Game Local Storage

Alchemy Sort stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This may include:

  • Current level number.
  • Accumulated gold coins.
  • Unlocked bottle skins and selected laboratory themes.
  • Sound volume and audio settings.

This game progress is stored locally and is not transmitted to or stored on our own external game servers. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App will delete locally stored progress and preferences.

The App is designed for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Third-party services, including advertising services, may process device or usage information as described in this policy and subject to applicable laws, settings, and consent requirements.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us using the email address in the Contact Us section.
