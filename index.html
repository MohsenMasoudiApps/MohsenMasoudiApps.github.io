Welcome to 2048 Fusion: Number Merge Puzzle ("we", "our", or the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent, safe, and enjoyable gaming experience.

We do not ask you to provide personally identifiable information such as your real name, physical address, email address, phone number, or contacts to play the App. Some third-party services integrated into the App may process certain technical information as described below.

2. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

  • No account creation: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide login credentials to play 2048 Fusion.
  • No location tracking: The App does not access or track your GPS or precise location.
  • No access to sensitive device features: The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, media library, or SMS messages.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the gameplay experience, the App stores certain game data locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore, including:

  • Game progress: Current board tile values, score, and move history for undo functionality.
  • Lifetime statistics: Highest score, total moves played, win/loss count, and unlocked tile achievements.
  • User preferences: Selected visual theme (for example, Midnight Dark or Cyber Neon), board grid size (3x3 up to 8x8), tile shape, and sound/haptic settings.

This gameplay data is stored locally and is not uploaded by the App to our own servers. If you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, locally stored data may be deleted.
