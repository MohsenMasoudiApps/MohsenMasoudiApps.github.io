This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Zen ("the App", "we", or "our") handles information when you use our application. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the information collected through the App.

1. Information Collection

No Account Required

Sudoku Zen does not require registration, login, or account creation. You do not need to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to play.

Local Game Data

Your puzzles, game progress, scores, statistics, preferences, and achievements are stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a custom backend or remote player-profile system for this game data.

You can generally remove locally stored data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. This does not necessarily delete information already processed by third-party providers.

The information processed depends on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable requirements.
